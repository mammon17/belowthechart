$Chinese is the slogan.
$MIHAO has the money.

“You met me at a very Chinese time in my life” is a meme from April 2025. A coin with that line was launched in September and slept at $40–65K. On Nov 11 the Solana “Chinese meta” started around a cat coin, $MIHAO, and this coin rode along to $365K. Then the crowd left. It has a famous phrase, a three-year-old X account and a thin pool. It does not have a reason of its own.

Question 1Is it the original?

Of the phrase, no. Of the coins, it’s the biggest by far.

  • The line parodies the end of Fight Club. Know Your Meme traces it to an X post from April 5, 2025, and TikTok to October 2025 (KYM). “Chinamaxxing” got coverage from NBC and NPR.
  • This coin was made on pump.fun on Sep 7 by 8ExM9dgr, a serial creator with 202 coins. It bought $627 with a 7-wallet bundle and sold $1.2K within ten minutes. None of the 15 launch bundles holds anything now.
  • 13 Solana coins share the name and 26 the ticker, most of them made on Nov 11. This one is two months older and much larger.
  • The X account @chinesetimeinmy was created in June 2022, three years before the meme: bought or renamed. 7.4K followers, blue check. The site chinesetimeinmylife.com is a fan page; CoinGecko lists a different site. No team, no roadmap, no CTO announcement.
