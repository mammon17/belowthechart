$Pokémon
the biggest brand, paired to CARDS

A pump.fun meme named after the biggest franchise in the world and paired not to SOL but to $CARDS, the Collector Crypt token for tokenized Pokémon cards. It ran to $4.2M in its first two days and has lived at $100–300K since. Every spike after that came from a CARDS listing or from the same handful of rotation wallets that buy thin pools and sell within hours. No affiliation with The Pokémon Company.

For

The original, not a clone: first coin with the name in our feeds, 24 copies came after it. A story that makes sense (the brand paired to the card token). One fresh wallet has been buying since yesterday and hasn’t sold. 6,800 holders and a community account that keeps posting.

Against

The cap is priced in CARDS, and CARDS is down about a third since its Upbit listing. Every spike since October was a few rotation wallets that sold within hours. Top-10 bags of ~$79K sit on ~$25K of quote liquidity. Today’s X posts are a bot campaign with 6–43 views each.

Odds of a new wave

Low. The CARDS listings are already played out. A real catalyst would be Collector Crypt itself naming $Pokémon, which has never happened. Without it, a $130–300K range or a slide toward $80–120K is more likely than a new high.

Origin

A good idea with nobody behind it

  • The idea. Pairing memes to a narrative token instead of SOL was a meta in October. The first callout reads like Cented’s circle (“they vamped cented”), but I didn’t confirm the Axiom name is that trader.
  • No dev to watch. The creator wallet is a shared launch-service deployer that made 184,906 coins, among them $Meridian. It holds 0%. There is no team; the X account is a community takeover with 314 followers.
  • Why CARDS matters. Collector Crypt runs a gacha and marketplace for tokenized Pokémon cards on Solana. The pool is $Pokémon / $CARDS, so the USD cap moves with CARDS even when nobody trades $Pokémon. 4pillars notes 72% of CARDS supply vests until November 2027.
  • The original. First coin with the name in our launch feeds; 24 clones followed within six hours. The only namesake on the coin list is a dead “OnlyPikachu” at $1.
  • The name. It uses a famous trademark. Whether the rights holder has ever reacted, I didn’t check. It is a real risk for a coin with that name.

Money

Flat flow, thin exit

  • Chasing spikes lost. Under a fixed plan (×2 or a −50% stop, 3% costs), buying the first wave at $1.8M, the Upbit spike at $310K, the rotation entry at $324K and the top of the leg at $438K all hit the stop. The only ×2 came from buying the dead bottom, which you only know in hindsight. One coin, not a statistic.
  • Quote-token coins do worse. In our launch data, coins paired to HYPE or TRUMP reached $100K about 5–7 times less often than coins paired to SOL. This one is already the exception; it doesn’t make the next leg likelier.

Bots talk, the crowd doesn’t
