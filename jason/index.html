How it got here

A launch from a tweetCreated on pump.fun on Oct 11 at 12:16 UTC, in the same second as a post by @sokoquant (113 followers). The creator 5zRLtaZU is a factory with 864 coins and holds 0. Bonded to PumpSwap on Oct 21. I couldn’t read the post: the X API timed out.
A fan page, not a team@JasonDuvalToken (59 followers) was made in March and renamed for the coin. Bio: “Paired with TTWO / NO DEV FEE”. A community takeover; no dev to blame, and nobody to hold the price.
Three days of crowdNov 12 22:28 → Nov 14 02:51: $26K → $176K. On Nov 14 22:05 a Telegram call (Wizzy’s Trades, 7.7K members) at $161K. Then $426K by Nov 15 and the high of $554K on Nov 16 at 03:23. Buys were $200–$4K per wallet; the top 5 net buyers were only about 20% of net buying, so nobody led it.

The pool holds 93.9M JASON against 100.8 TTWO. Ten wallets selling their 198M tokens, more than the whole pool, would get about 68 TTWO (~$14K) and drop the price ~90%. Any seller of $5K moves it ~20%. Rough x·y=k on the main pool only.

The quote is a stockThe pool is JASON/TTWO. The dollar price of the coin is its TTWO price times Take-Two’s stock token, ~$207.6 now. If the stock sells off on release news, the coin loses in dollars even if nobody sells it. I didn’t check how that token trades on the day.
Rivals at the gateThere are 42 coins with the $JASON ticker on Solana. One of them trades against TTWO, wKy6tEDZ ($64K), pushed on X as “real”. Three fresh copies appeared overnight, and the launches below are promised. In the same meta, $GTA6 is worth ~$15M and $Lucia ~$0.8M. Check the CA.
