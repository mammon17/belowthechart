$José
one Elon post made it, only another can save it

On Nov 6 Elon talked about the “José” meme on stage: one man digs, eight watch. A coin launched 1.5 hours after the full video went up, ran, then faded to $41K. A day later Elon posted “I want to be José” himself, and it went ×40 in 15 minutes. Now money is flowing out, and the only engine left is Elon’s attention.

For

Elon posted the meme himself, to 1.96M views. This is the biggest José on Solana by far; the clones are a fraction of its size. Launch bundles and the creator are fully out. A few big wallets are still adding, including one with +$168K in 30 days.

Against

One driver and nothing else: no team, no X account, no roadmap. Net outflow of $61K in 24 hours. The biggest buyer of the morning bounce just sold 98 SOL. The top 10 holders sit on ~$155K against ~$37K of SOL in the pool. The ticker is buried in drainer spam.

Odds of a new wave

Low without a new Elon post. High if he mentions José again: last time money arrived in the same second. Nobody can predict when, or if, that happens.

Origin

An interview line, not a team

There is no dev to follow here. The story is Elon’s, and the coin is the market’s bet on it.

  • The source is an interview. Elon spoke at the Baron Investment Conference on Nov 6. People quoting it on X (@x2kekiusmaximus, @japan_nobunaga, 487K) have him describing the meme and saying he aspires to be José. Grok places the quote in the same interview. I did not watch the clip myself.
  • A launchpad, not a person. The creator wallet bwamJzzt has created 183,496 tokens. It bought $1.1K, sold $3.3K in the first minutes and holds nothing. The token’s website is otcdesks.cash, and @OTCTerminal brags about it as a top mover. The coin has no X account and no site of its own.
  • The tweet in the token profile came later. @ZefWasHere (3.9K followers, 18K views) posted 38 minutes after launch. A promoter, not the origin.
  • Why this one. Three “Be Like José” coins were created on pump.fun in the same minute (AHaRC65E $2.9K, SAiDhqEU $2.8K). Only 87CQ survived. The main Solana rival is 5NRzuqfy, paired with MUSK4, at $134K. Dozens of copies on Robinhood chain, Arc, BSC and ETH appeared after Elon’s post. @BagLordGogi pushes 87CQ as the “correctly spelled” José.
