$CHOO
a real dog, real advisors, and 1% bags

Choo is a rescue dog, the face of a pet-longevity brand run by a founder with 225K followers. The coin ran ×3 and ×4 on two advisor announcements, peaked at $5.8M on Nov 3, and is down about 70% since. On-chain, the dev’s bag went out by transfer, seven wallets got exactly 1% each, and the sellers are winning.

For

A public founder with 225K followers and a real business behind the dog. The dog’s account is 7 months older than the coin. A concrete catalyst is promised: pay for Pawgen orders in $CHOO, with the tokens burned. “Soon”, no date.

Against

The dev’s 2% left by transfer, not by sells. Seven wallets got exactly 1% each, assembled from two distributor wallets, one of which sold into the pool at both peaks. Bundlers hold 31%. In 24 hours the main pool saw 2,415 SOL sold vs 992 bought. Smart money is out.

Odds of a new wave

Medium, and only on a catalyst: Pawgen payments with visible burns, a CEX listing, or another big advisor. Realistic target ×1.5–2. Without one, a slide to $1.2–1.5M is more likely.

Origin

A real dog with a business behind it

  • The founder is public. @morellifit (Michael Morelli): on X since 2011, 225K followers. Bio: “Founder of FitScript, NorthBlu, Reverra, pawgen… $CHOO project & IP”. He writes about peptides, GLP-1 and longevity, and posts about Choo daily.
  • The dog. Abandoned twice, blind in one eye, 102 lb at age 9; now 14 and “running again on peptides”. Pitched as “the most measured dog alive”, a nod to Bryan Johnson. Shillers cite a Business Insider story about it. I could not find that story.
  • The account is not a copy. @ChooTheRescue was created in March 2025, seven months before the coin: 3K followers, blue check, CA in bio. Site chootherescue.com, Telegram t.me/chootherescue. 99 DexScreener boosts.
  • The dev is a pro launcher. @AresSprout (19K): “Founder @conviction_x | @chootherescue dev”. GMGN counts 84 tokens announced from that account.
  • The promise. 1% of every trade goes to dog rescue, and paying for Pawgen orders in $CHOO burns tokens (per the site). Neither is visible on-chain yet.
  • The original. Nine earlier $CHOO coins on Solana were empty “choo” clones under $8K with no link to Morelli. This is the first “Choo Choo” tied to the IP owner.
