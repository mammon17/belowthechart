$AVENGERS
a year-old ticker waiting for a trailer

AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY was made on pump.fun in March 2025, hours after a film-news tweet, and sat at $2–10K for over a year. It finally bonded on Nov 3 and touched $72K on Nov 9. There is no team, no whale and almost no X. The whole bet is the Marvel film on Dec 18 and the trailer rumored before it.

For

The oldest coin with the film’s exact name. A dated catalyst: the release on Dec 18, with a new trailer rumored for Nov 10–25. US presales are past $50M. At $54K the cap is tiny next to the theme, and the dev is long gone, so there is no dev bag to dump.

Against

Liquidity is ~$15K. One holder with 8% would knock the price down ~55% by selling. The address has been posted on X four times in total, all by bots. Buys and sells are even, and a sandwich bot makes ~20% of the volume. The last two official trailers gave only ×2.

Odds of a new wave

Medium, and only on the trailer. If Marvel drops it and buyers show up in the first minutes, $100–150K is realistic. Without it, a slow range of $40–70K until December is the base case.

Origin

No team, just a title

  • The dev left long ago. BD6WZMo3 was funded from Binance, created this one token, bought $136, sold $145 and holds 0. No CTO, no account, no website. The “twitter” field points to the DiscussingFilm tweet.
  • The original, for the full name only. It is the only coin called “AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY”, and the oldest with the film’s exact title. Holders pitch it as “OG 1yr+, never bonded, exact logo”. But the $AVENGERS ticker has many namesakes: ByCsUXz2 (Oct 17, called at $5K→$86K), 3EaYnwiP (Oct 18), “Asian Avengers”, “Avengers Chihuahua”, “Redpill Avengers”, plus copies on other chains. Check the CA.
  • The film is real and big. Avengers: Doomsday opens in the US on Dec 18, 2026 (THR). Disney says US presales passed $50M (report). The interest is huge. Almost none of it reaches the coin.
  • The sister coin. $DOOM trades the same film at ~$450–520K. Callers sell $AVENGERS as its cheap beta: “if OG Doom hit 1 million, this will be at 100k at least”.

Money

Even flow, thin exit

  • Neither accumulation nor distribution. 135 buyers in 24 hours, most of them small. No buying on a schedule since the migration.
  • Sellers of the day: an early holder sold $2.1K and is out (bought $343 at $7–10K, ~×6); 856qbbPX, an August holder, $634; 4czhB1Xj $499 this morning.
  • Where the money was made. Buying before the bond (under $10K) or on the Nov 5 low worked: ×2 within a day or two. Every entry at $45–72K after the migration ended between −42% and +12% after 72 hours. One coin, not a statistic.

X and Telegram

Crypto X hasn’t found it

In its whole life the contract address has been posted on X four times, and the biggest of those posts got 501 views. No KOL, no organic thread. The coin lives on fomo and Axiom: 22 callouts from 13 authors since Oct 17, one in the last 24 hours (“send it”, from a top-10 holder).
