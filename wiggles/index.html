03

Diagnosis

Made by a crowd, not a whaleEvery run had hundreds of buyers at $9–18 each. No wallet organized it; the dev cashed out ~$350 in the first seconds and left.
A third of the volume isn’t demandOver 6 hours, ~30% of turnover came from wallets that bought and sold about the same amount: sandwich bots like MriyaNN8 and ARu4n5mF, and a fresh wallet Fz4TSaTJ with 462 trades.
Holder rewards: a soft floorpump.fun pays the creator fee to everyone holding $20+ every few minutes, in WIGGLES, from GmVnEE8L (how it works). As volume shrinks, so do the payouts.
The X account can get bannedIts feed has racist and antisemitic posts. A banned token account usually means a dump. The coin has no product, no date and no owner of the meme to fall back on.
Paid reach came late@MaxelRowan (126K) posted a text that @CryptoDominion_ repeated word for word later; three accounts posted in the same minute with ~28 likes each; “48X → 288X” bot callers. Most of it landed after the top.
The original, for nowFirst coin with the name in our feeds, oldest of six on Solana. Copies on Base and Robinhood-chain are already dead.
