The team posted the CA themselves, the dev locked his whole bag for a hundred years, and the crowd took it ×94 in two hours. Since the top, buying has dropped about ten times.

Security check

✓Dev locked, never sold. CrSbohT2 sent all 10M into a Streamflow lock 68 seconds after launch (announced). Creator fees go to the same wallet; the team says for marketing.
✓The project posted the CA. @SocksDotFun has teased the card since Oct 16 and shows card demos and Apple Pay payments.
✓Bags are spread out. The biggest wallet outside the pool holds about 2%. Launch snipers sold within seconds.
✕Someone else issues the cards. The team says a third-party provider does. Which one, and whether it allows no-KYC cards, is unknown.
✕No-KYC cards get shut down. SolCard’s bank partner forced KYC and froze anonymous cards (The Defiant).

Promo or people?

  • Bots: three ~7.8K-follower accounts posted the same $SOCKS text within 2 seconds (26–42 views); two “analysis” posts went out 3 seconds apart; “123x on my call” and “DM for VIP” callers; the same “some dude paid for water with his socks card” line from three names in the GMGN chat.
  • People: sober breakdowns of the fees (issue $15, plus $1 and 4% per top-up), and real users showing payments. No organic post from an account above 50K followers.
  • Copies: five “Socks Neobank” clones on Solana within hours, ~20 on Robinhood-chain. The original has the X account and the site. Check the CA.
  • 1,420 Axiom callouts from 559 authors, 19 Telegram calls.
