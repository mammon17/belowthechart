Origin

A real dog, an unofficial coin

Mamesuke is the shiba mascot of the TV Osaka show 「豆助のワンにゃん総本家」, now on its 26th dog. The TV channel has nothing to do with the coin.

  • The official account @mamesuke_tvo (since 2020, 67K followers) had a viral Halloween post on Oct 31: 18,285 likes, 255K views. It also announced the Nov 3 episode. It never mentioned the coin and has been silent since Nov 3.
  • The coin’s account @MamesukeShow was created 26 minutes before launch. 2,064 followers, “Airing every other Tuesday”, dog pictures 4–8 times a day at 1–10K views. Site themamesuke.com, Telegram t.me/mamesukeTG.
  • Not the first $MAMESUKE. Three were launched on Robinhood chain on Nov 1, and an older pump.fun namesake 94RNfQN5…pump (@MAMESUKESOL, ~$16–20K) is pushed as the “OG”. 13 coins share the ticker on Solana. This one is the biggest, not the first.
  • Platform: StonkFun, “standard” mode. Trading fees go to the dev; holders get no rewards.
