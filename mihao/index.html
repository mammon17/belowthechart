For

Ansem bought on-chain, said so himself, and spent the night asking other big accounts to put the cat on their avatars. Dozens did. The dev is a public trader who airdropped to his community at launch and has not sold in 16 days. The meme is everywhere outside crypto.

Against

×47 in two days. Hourly volume fell from $12.3M to under $1M within five hours. The dev’s 8.95% is worth $1.2M, three times the liquidity of all pools. Bundler-tagged wallets hold 35.5%. Ansem’s wallet is −$304K over 30 days, and on $CYPHERCAT he sold two hours after buying.

Odds of a new wave

Medium, and only while the avatar chain grows. If a few more 300K+ accounts switch and volume returns above $3M an hour, $25–40M is in reach. With no dated catalyst, a range of $8–15M is the base case for the next day.

Origin

A meme with no owner

  • The phrase. “You met me at a very Chinese time in my life” is a Fight Club parody first posted in April 2025 that turned into the 2026 “becoming Chinese” trend (Know Your Meme, Complex). The cat is said to be “Sasha” from a 2023 TikTok with a white-beard filter. I didn’t verify that.
  • The dev is public. @faceofsol (“RaRa”, 6.2K followers, 61K posts) ranks on pump.fun’s own @PumpfunEco “bagworkers” board at $1,000,000. He created 6 coins by GMGN’s count.
  • The kit. Coin account @iAmNihaoCat (3.9K), avatar factory @mihaopfps created Nov 7, site mihao.fun with an avatar section. The avatar mechanic was built before Ansem showed up.
  • The original. First coin of the theme in our launch feeds. Eight Solana namesakes share the ticker; this is by far the largest. The oldest, “Ni Hao Cat” 25YFutpW from May 2025, sits at $12.7K. Satellites are already out: $babymihao, “$yunmi cat”. Check the CA.

Money

The fire is cooling, the bags are big

  • Where the money was made. Entering one minute after the Ansem-tagged buy at $2.4M, or one minute after his “vibe shift” post at $7.4M, both reached ×2 under a fixed plan (x2 or a −40% stop). The 24-hour window hasn’t closed yet, so these are early numbers. One coin, not a statistic.
  • Paid noise. Callers boasting “398x since my telegram call” and “658x from my call” with giveaways, paid “listings” and smart-money-sold bots arrived after the top. None of that is demand.
