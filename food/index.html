$FOOD: the platform cooked this one

A pump.fun charity coin: every creator fee goes to Feeding America through donate.gg, locked in the contract. It went from $9K to $2.5M in seven hours, and the last ×5 came in 13 minutes after pump.fun’s co-founder reposted donate.gg’s founder with “let’s win”. The crowd is huge. The net money is zero.

03

Where the fees go

The contract

100% of creator fees to Feeding America

Set at launch and locked: the creator gets nothing. The recipient wallet is BBEEdQHRTvNCEM8gXeLrFShpAVHnUsWGoEJr9Pj8sV8j. It has not claimed anything yet.

The claims

“$20,339”, then “1,000,000 meals”

The coin’s account posts fundraising updates. I didn’t match those numbers on-chain, and I found no statement from Feeding America itself about being a partner.

The platform

pump.fun Charity Coins, since April

A feature built with donate.gg (KuCoin News). A critic says donate.gg keeps 10% (open letter). Earlier charity coins topped out at $1.6–1.9M; this one is already above.

  • About 73K buys and 58K sells in 24 hours. Axiom traders, sandwich bots and quick flippers make most of the buying. The top 5 buyers are only ~10% of it: no single wallet is carrying this.
  • After the top, sellers had the edge: −$46K net from 04:20 to 04:39. Nobody dumped; big holders are trimming in pieces.
  • The main pool is thin for a $2M coin. Ten pools in total, including Meteora, spread the exit a little; I only modeled the main one.
