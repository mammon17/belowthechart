Origin

A tweet, then a coin 14 seconds later

On November 6 the pump.fun app got a new 💎 Diamond tab under Callout Rewards, next to the old “Degen” one. People who retold the announcement say it rewards callouts on bigger coins and holding, and penalizes spam. The coin is named after it and trades against $PUMP, not SOL.

  • First source: @5iZedup at 00:28:23 UTC: “Pumpfun added this diamond to the callout page? Is this a multiplier, or a new season? $Diamond paired w pump”. 499 followers, 13.7K views. The coin appeared 14 seconds later, and its X link points to this exact tweet. The account was created in March 2024 and was called @thejewmeta and @sp1dersol before. Its link to the dev wallet is not proven.
  • Confirmation from pump.fun: at 01:18 UTC @initwithcarl, the pump.fun mobile app lead (6.7K followers), wrote that they are testing experimental algorithms next to the current one, with a small share of the daily rewards pool. 33K views. Wave 2 started right on this post. Summaries: @uslybtc.
  • Background: Callout Rewards have paid USDC for callouts since August 13. On October 10 rewards were tied to the profits of people who follow a caller (Crypto Briefing).
  • Original, not a clone: the first and only “Diamond” on Solana. Clones appeared later on Robinhood chain, 8+ of them; the biggest, 0x3C60…8a82, reached $215K. A Chinese post by @seymataan calls it “a meme on the platform’s meta” and says similar waves came at the August launch and the October change.
  • Community takeover: DexScreener shows a CTO, links updated at 02:48 UTC. 100% of creator fees go to CnYJQ33…fVAN (locked, not claimed yet). A Chinese post says the creator rewards go to holders. Who owns that wallet is not checked.
