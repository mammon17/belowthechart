$WEALTH
a public dev, a thin pool, one big holder

A “everyone wants wealth” meme from a dev who signs his name to it. From a $52K low on Nov 6 to $1.34M in under two days, then a 3× flash crash and a full recovery in 35 minutes. Here is what holds the price up, and what could knock it over.

For

A public dev who locked his 2% on day one and never sold. Launch bundles and snipers are fully out. The dev posts every day and promises “absolutely huge announcements” this week. Each wave so far was ×2 to ×8.

Against

The pool is thin: three sells of ~86 SOL halved the price in five minutes. The top 10 holders sit on ~$190K against ~$38K of SOL in the pool. Hourly volume is fading, and the shilling is at the “vote for listing” and phishing-bot stage.

Odds of a new wave

Medium. It needs a real announcement and volume back above $40–50K an hour. It holds only as long as the biggest wallet keeps holding.

Origin

A dev with a name and a news package

The dev is public: @redsol23, bio “$USELESS team | $WEALTH dev”. The launch post was pinned in the $USELESS X community (61K members). The whole story is “the $USELESS team launched a new coin”.

  • The dev locked, not sold. Creator wallet GkR7sLwP (first token ever, funded from Bybit) bought exactly 2% for $53 and three minutes later sent all of it to a Streamflow lock 8cBcJH2G (tx). It matches his “20M locked forever” post. No other transfers or sells from the creator. The unlock date was not checked.
  • A news package at minute seven. @coldtrz (84K) at 15:11:26 and @BSCNews (1.37M) one second later, same text. Then @WhaleInsider (678K), the dev’s own “Wealth Coin is live”, @Core_002 (1M) and @BitcoinIntelX (163K). Two “news” posts one second apart, seven minutes after launch, look prepaid. There is no direct proof.
  • He has done this before. On Oct 22 the same dev launched $MOON, and the same accounts covered it as news. How $MOON ended was not checked. That is about one new coin every two weeks.
  • The $USELESS link rests on the dev’s own bio, the community pin and the news accounts. No official $USELESS confirmation was found. @Darecomes: “Not same team but same claims”.
  • The original. The biggest of 10 $WEALTH coins on Solana and the first in this wave. Copies appeared on Nov 6–7 on ETH, Base, Arc and Robinhood chain, and on Solana (2pXkve4s $289K, 3uMiHzaN $127K). Holders themselves warn there is no $WEALTH on Ethereum.
  • The coin’s account @solwealthcoin was created on Nov 7: 956 followers, 4 posts, blue check. Site wealthcoinsol.com since Nov 6. A paid DexScreener ad on launch night, no boost.

What next

Three ways it goes

New wave, medium odds$1.5–2.5M
  • the dev ships a concrete announcement (collab, CEX listing) in 1–3 days
  • hourly volume back above $40–50K
  • holds $700K, then breaks $1.07M and the $1.34M high
  • top 1 keeps holding
Range, most likely$500–900K
  • announcements turn out small: aggregators, AMAs, spaces
  • volume keeps fading, single wallets move the price
  • more 40–60% wicks down with fast buybacks, like Nov 7
Dumpbelow $400K, then $200K
  • top 1 or 2–3 of the top 10 sell
  • the dev moves on to a new coin ($MOON → $WEALTH took 14 days)
  • $USELESS publicly denies the link
  • with a $77K pool this takes minutes

Levels and exit signs

  • $1.07M and $1.34M: a break on volume means the next wave. $546K (Nov 8 morning low): a break down ends the range case. $221–424K: the crash low.
  • Leave if top 1 or 2+ of the top 10 sell, 6-hour net flow drops below −$20K, the dev starts posting another ticker, or pool liquidity falls under $60K.
  • Hourly volume under $10K at $800K means it is fading.
